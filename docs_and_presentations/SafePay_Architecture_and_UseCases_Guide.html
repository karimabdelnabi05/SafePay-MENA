How The Dynamic Risk Score Is Calculated

Non-linear combinatorial math that protects against false alarms while catching real attackers.

Master Formula
RiskScore = min(100, max(0, [Base + SIM_Decay(t) + RelativeAmount + Multipliers] − TrustReservoir))
1. SIM Time Decay

SIM swaps are not binary 48h walls:

  • • < 1 hour ago: +85 pts
  • • 24 hours ago: +25 pts
  • • 40 hours ago: +5 pts
2. Relative Ratio

Personalized to user habits:

  • • Ratio = Amount ÷ User Avg
  • • Ratio < 2.0x: +0 pts
  • • Ratio > 20x: +20 pts
3. Force Multipliers

Toxic combinations explode score:

  • • New IMEI phone: 1.5x
  • • 3+ Tx in 5 mins: 1.4x
  • • Rogue Roaming: +20 pts
4. Trust Reservoir

Absorbs harmless anomalies:

  • • Account age > 1 yr: -25 pts
  • • Known hardware: -30 pts
  • • 50+ past clean tx: -20 pts
