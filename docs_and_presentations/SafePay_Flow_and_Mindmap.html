Wall 1: The Login Gate

The hacker must install the app on his phone. SafePay detects the SIM was swapped 1h ago on an unknown IMEI, putting the entire session into a Restricted Security Mode before transfer #1.

Wall 2: The New Payee Gate

The hacker must send money to a stranger's burner account. SafePay enforces a mandatory Central Bank cooling-off limit and Face ID challenge on all fresh recipients.

Wall 3: The Velocity Tripwire

If an attacker tries rapid micro-transfers, SafePay's 60-second velocity tripwire detects the unnatural burst frequency and locks the account on transfer #2.
