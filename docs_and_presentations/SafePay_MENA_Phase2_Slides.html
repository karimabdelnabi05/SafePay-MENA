The decision gap

Payment systems and mobile networks see different pieces of the same attack

The payment provider sees

  • Amount and recipient relationship
  • Payment channel and customer history
  • Session and transaction anomalies
  • Customer-reported scam context

The mobile network can observe

  • Recent SIM change
  • Number association through supported authorization
  • Recent device change
  • Roaming and reachability status

SafePay orchestrates those observations as evidence. It does not treat any single signal as proof of fraud or payment intent.

Agentic AI

The agent chooses evidence; policy controls consequences

Context-aware selection

The model receives transaction facts and pre-screen reasons, never the canned scenario label.

Bounded execution

Five allowlisted tools, fixed schemas, no model-selected phone or URL, no repeat calls, and a hard evidence budget.

Fail-closed policy

Missing required evidence prevents release. Independently supported blocks remain blocked; the model cannot fabricate an observation.

Accepted tool calls, evidence status, summarized failures, and the final policy source are exposed in the result trace.

Model: Gemini function calling; network: Nokia sandbox7 / 10
